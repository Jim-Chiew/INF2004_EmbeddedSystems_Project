Test: buddy1_mqtt on a Pico W, home WiFi, Mosquitto broker on a Windows laptop
Date: 2026-10-15
Procedure: the program runs with a broker; the broker is stopped and restarted
several times. One sample is taken per second and goes through the offline queue.
Observed:
 - First period (13 s to 70 s): broker unreachable (Windows firewall), state
   DISCONNECTED, queue grows by 1 per second.
 - Reconnect at about 476 s after a much longer outage: queue was at its limit
   (94 to 97 of 100), dropped=367, state SYNC. Queue drained (acked rose),
   state CONNECTED, dropped reset to 0 (FR-COM-09 to 12).
 - Outage at about 543 s to 604 s (broker stopped for ~1 min): DISCONNECTED,
   queue grew 1 per second to 61, dropped=0. On reconnect: SYNC, queue drained
   to 1, CONNECTED, acked rose 1 per second.
 - Broker stopped again at about 650 s: DISCONNECTED within 1 s.
Note: excerpts of the serial output. The gaps (70 s to 476 s, 521 s to 550 s)
are where the serial monitor was closed and reopened.
